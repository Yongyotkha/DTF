Subject: The Notification on the Essential Facts and Laws of Anti-Dumping Final Determination on Flat Hot Rolled Steel in Coils and not in Coils Originating in the Socialist Republic of Vietnam and the Arab Republic of Egypt.

Reference is made to the letter of No. 0309/3047 dated 1st December B.E. 2564 (2021) informing Your Excellency the Notification of Final Determination to impose Anti-Dumping Measure on imports of Flat Hot Rolled Steel in Coils and not in Coils Originating in the Socialist Republic of Vietnam and the Arab Republic of Egypt.

Pursuant to Ministerial Regulation on The Notification Essential Facts and Laws of Anti-Dumping Final Determination by virtue of the Anti-Dumping and Countervailing Act B.E. 2542 and Anti-Dumping and Countervailing Act (Amendment) B.E. 2562 and Article 12.2 of WTO Anti-Dumping Agreement, Department of Foreign Trade (DFT) would like to inform Your Excellency that the DFT issued a notification to publicize the essential facts and laws of the anti-dumping final determination on Flat Hot Rolled Steel in Coils and not in Coils Originating in the Socialist Republic of Vietnam and the Arab Republic of Egypt B.E. 2564 (2021), announced in the Royal Thai Government Gazette on 11th January B.E. 2565 (2022) as enclosed herewith.        

Please accept, Excellency, the assurances of my highest consideration.
