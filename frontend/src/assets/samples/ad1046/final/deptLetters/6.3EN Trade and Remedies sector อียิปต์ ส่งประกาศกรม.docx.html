Subject: The Notification on the Essential Facts and Laws of Anti-Dumping Final Determination on Flat Hot Rolled Steel in Coils and not in Coils Originating in the Socialist Republic of Vietnam and the Arab Republic of Egypt.

Reference is made to the Technical and Legal Statement of Egypt from Trade Remedies Sector, Ministry of Trade and Industry, the Arab Republic of Egypt dated 28th September B.E. 2564 (2021) commenting on the Draft Final Determination of Anti-Dumping Investigation on Flat Hot Rolled Steel in Coils and not in Coils Originating in the Socialist Republic of Vietnam and the Arab Republic of Egypt.

Pursuant to Ministerial Regulation on The Notification Essential Facts and Laws of Anti-Dumping Final Determination by virtue of the Anti-Dumping and Countervailing Act B.E. 2542 and Anti-Dumping and Countervailing Act (Amendment) B.E. 2562 and Article 12.2 of WTO Anti-Dumping Agreement, Department of Foreign Trade (DFT) would like to inform you that the DFT issued a notification to publicize the essential facts and laws of the anti-dumping final determination on Flat Hot Rolled Steel in Coils and not in Coils Originating in the Socialist Republic of Vietnam and the Arab Republic of Egypt B.E. 2564 (2021), announced in the Royal Thai Government Gazette on 11th January B.E. 2565 (2022) as enclosed herewith.
