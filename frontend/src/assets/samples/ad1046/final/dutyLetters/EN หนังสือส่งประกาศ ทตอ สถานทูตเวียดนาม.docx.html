Subject: Notification of Final Determination to impose Anti-Dumping Measure on imports of Flat Hot Rolled Steel in Coils and not in Coils Originating in the Socialist Republic of Vietnam and the Arab Republic of Egypt.

Reference is made to the letter of the Department of Foreign Trade (DFT) No. 0309/2285 dated 10th September B.E. 2564 (2021) informing the disclosure of the essential facts for the draft final determination of an anti-dumping investigation on Flat Hot Rolled Steel in Coils and not in Coils Originating in the Socialist Republic of Vietnam and the Arab Republic of Egypt.

In this connection, we would like to inform Your Excellency that the Committee on Dumping and Subsidy has made a final determination to impose AD measure on imports of Flat Hot Rolled Steel in Coils and not in Coils Originating in the Socialist Republic of Vietnam and the Arab Republic of Egypt classified under the Thai Customs Tariff code (HS codes 2017), total of 133 HS codes by applying anti-dumping duty at the rate of 4.74% – 42.34% of CIF price in order to prevent the emergence of material injury to the domestic industry.

Pursuant to the Anti-Dumping and Countervailing Act B.E. 2542 and Article 12.2 of WTO Anti-Dumping Agreement, the Committee’s notification of final determination of an anti-dumping duty on the product concerned was announced and publicized in the Royal Thai Government Gazette on 30th November B.E. 2564 (2021) as enclosed herewith.


Please accept, Excellency, the assurances of my highest consideration.
