Subject: Disclosure of the Essential Facts for the Draft Final Determination of Anti-Dumping Investigation on Flat Hot Rolled Steel in Coils and not in Coils Originating in the Socialist Republic of Vietnam and the Arab Republic of Egypt.

Reference is made to the letter of the Department of Foreign Trade (DFT) No. 0309/3185 dated 23rd November B.E. 2563 (2020) informing the initiation of an anti-dumping investigation on Flat Hot Rolled Steel in Coils and not in Coils Originating in the Socialist Republic of Vietnam and the Arab Republic of Egypt.

Pursuant to Article 30 of the Thailand’s Anti-Dumping and Countervailing Act B.E. 2542, the DFT hereby disclosed the essential facts for the draft final determination of anti-dumping investigation as enclosed herewith (Attachment 1).

To comply with the decision made by Thai government to prevent the spread of Corona Virus or COVID-19 by avoiding mass gathering of people, the DFT hereby inform Your Excellency that

  • Should Your Excellency wish the DFT to host an oral hearing or to attend such hearing, kindly provide name list of the attendees (not more than 2 persons) in the Acceptance Form for oral hearing (Attachment 2).  Furthermore, should Your Excellency also wish to provide comment on the draft final determination of the aforementioned anti-dumping investigation, kindly submit written comments in both non-confidential (required) and confidential versions (optional). The deadline to receive all aforementioned documents is on Thursday 23rd September B.E. 2564 (2021) at 16.30 Bangkok time via tird1@moc.go.th.
  • In case there is any interested party requests an oral hearing, the DFT will host one via Zoom Application on Tuesday 28th September B.E. 2564 (2021) at 10.00 Bangkok Time with Meeting ID: 969 7744 8333 and Passcode: AD1046. The rules of an oral hearing procedure are herewith attached (Attachment 3).




  • Should any additional information orally presented during the oral hearing that Your Excellency wish the DFT to take into consideration, kindly submit written comments in both non-confidential (required) and confidential versions (optional) and submit to the DFT by Friday 1st October B.E. 2564 (2021) at 16.30 Bangkok time via tird1@moc.go.th.

Any Submission after the aforementioned deadlines may be disregarded in accordance with Article 27 of the Thailand’s Anti-Dumping and Countervailing Act B.E. 2542.
