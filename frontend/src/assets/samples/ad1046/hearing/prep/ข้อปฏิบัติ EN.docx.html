Rules of Oral Hearing Procedure

Regarding the Draft Final Determination of Anti-Dumping Investigation on 

Flat Hot Rolled Steel in Coils and not in Coils

originating in the Socialist Republic of Vietnam and the Arab Republic of Egypt

Tuesday 28th September 2021, 10.00 a.m. (Bangkok time)

via Zoom Application

Meeting ID: 969 7744 8333 Passcode: AD1046


Any interested party wishing to present the evidences, views or comments during the hearing is required to submit acceptance form for oral hearing and the written statements in non-confidential (required) and confidential (optional) versions signed by the authorized person and affixed with the company seal by Thursday 23rd September 2021, 04.30 p.m. (Bangkok time).


  • The Department of Foreign Trade will not respond or answer any questions that might be raised by the interested parties during the hearing.
  • The hearing will be conducted in Thai, presentation in English is acceptable.
  • Interested party will be allowed to present evidences, views or comments one at a time and each will be given 15 minutes time allotment. The Chairperson is within his/her rights to stop any representatives who spend more than the specified time limit. The sequence of presentations will be notified on the date of the hearing.
  • The VDO and audio will be recorded.
  • The Chairperson is entitled to amend any regulations that are not initially mentioned in the Rules during the hearing, whenever deemed appropriate.
  • Any additional views, comments and/or information, apart from initially submitted documents, which might be raised during the hearing and would like to be used as evidences for consideration, shall be submitted in written form in non-confidential (required) and confidential (optional) versions signed by the authorized person and affixed with the company seal to the Department of Foreign Trade by Friday 1st October 2021, 04.30 p.m.
