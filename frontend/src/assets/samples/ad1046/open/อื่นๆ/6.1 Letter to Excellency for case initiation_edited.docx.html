Subject : The Initiation of an Anti-dumping Proceeding on Certain Iron or Steel Pipe and Tube Originating in the Socialist Republic of Vietnam


       The Department of Foreign Trade (“DFT”) wishes to refer to the DFT’s letter No. 0309/2966 dated 1 August 2018 notifying the initiation of an anti-dumping proceeding on aforementioned subject and the procedure on selection of samples of Vietnamese exporting producers.

The Government of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam is hereby notified that the DFT has initiated a of an anti-dumping proceeding on Certain Iron or Steel Pipe and Tube (“the subjected goods”) originating in the Socialist Republic of Vietnam, under Thai Customs Classification Codes (HS codes 2017), total of 169 HS codes. (Descriptions of HS codes are in an Attachment.) The notice has been announced in Royal Gazette on….August 2018.

The following documents are attached herewith and already dispatched directly to the known exporting producers and exporters in the Socialist Republic of Vietnam, as presented in an attached list of known exporting producers and exporters. 

1. A copy of the notification.

2. A non-confidential version of complaint.

3. A pre-questionnaire for exporting producers/exporters.

4. A CD-ROM contained the pre-questionnaire.

In this regard, the Embassy is kindly requested to forward the pre-questionnaire and other relevant documents to known exporting producers and exporters of the subjected merchandise in the Socialist Republic of Vietnam, other than those included in the aforementioned list. It is important for concerned exporting producers and exporters to complete the pre-questionnaire and furnish related information. The questionnaire responses shall be received by the DFT before 4:30 p.m., Bangkok time on…………………….… (14 calendar days from the date of this letter).

Please accept, Excellency, the renewed assurances of my highest consideration.
